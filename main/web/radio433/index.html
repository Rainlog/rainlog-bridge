Home Wi-Fi

Bridge Wi-Fi

Stays on while devices are connected, during setup, or if Home Wi-Fi is down. Restart the bridge, press its setup button, or open this page through Home Wi-Fi to wake it. Home Wi-Fi and weather radio keep working.

Idle shutdown is disabled because a Wi-Fi weather uploader is configured. Bridge Wi-Fi must stay available for it to reconnect.

Also unlocks this page from your home network: browse to the bridge's Home IP (shown on its screen) and sign in with this password.
